Your Google files, available to your own local assistant

Agent connects the owner's Google account to an assistant running on the owner's computer. It works with Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides only when the owner asks.

Google Drive

Find, read and organize the owner's files and folders.

Google Docs

Read and edit documents on request.

Google Sheets

Read and update spreadsheet data.

Google Slides

Read and edit presentations.

Not a public service

The app is used only by its owner. Tokens stay on the owner's computer and no data is sent to a third-party server. Read the privacy policy.
